## PairingNet

**Image-fragment pair searching and matching · ECCV 2024 · Python**

PairingNet combines contour and neighboring-texture information to search for related image fragments and infer matching boundaries. The repository includes the official implementation and links to generated and real-fragment datasets.

[Code](https://github.com/zhourixin/PairingNet){: .btn .btn--primary }
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08704){: .btn }
[Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhourixin/Fragments-dataset){: .btn }

## Multi-Granularity Archaeological Dating of Chinese Bronze Dings

**Fine-grained recognition · CVPR 2023 · Python**

An end-to-end multi-head network and knowledge-guided relation graph for predicting the era of Chinese bronze dings at multiple levels of granularity.

[Code](https://github.com/zhourixin/bronze-Ding){: .btn .btn--primary }
[Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhou_Multi-Granularity_Archaeological_Dating_of_Chinese_Bronze_Dings_Based_on_a_CVPR_2023_paper.html){: .btn }

## ShiftedBronzes

**Open-world recognition · Fine-grained distribution shifts**

A benchmark for studying out-of-distribution behavior in specialized fine-grained domains, centered on bronze-ware dating under several forms of real-world distribution shift.

[Code](https://github.com/zhourixin/ShiftedBronzes){: .btn .btn--primary }
